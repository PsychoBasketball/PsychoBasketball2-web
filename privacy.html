Psycho Basketball

Privacy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Psycho Basketball is a free browser game made by one developer. This page says what the game keeps and what it does not.

The open world game (psychobasketball.com/hood)

  • Your progress is saved in your own browser (browser storage on your device). It never leaves your device. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.
  • No account, no sign-in, no email. The game does not ask who you are.
  • No analytics and no trackers on the game page. The page loads only its own files. No third-party script runs inside it.
  • No advertising.
  • If the game is played on a games portal (for example CrazyGames or Poki), that portal's own privacy policy covers the portal's page, ads and accounts. The game itself still keeps nothing but your local progress.

The arcade game (psychobasketball.com)

  • The arcade page uses Google Analytics to count visits and which mode is played. Google may set cookies for that; see Google's privacy policy.
  • It offers optional sign-in with Google, Telegram or Discord for cross-device saves. If you sign in, the game receives the identifier and display name that provider gives, and nothing else. You can play without signing in.

Children

The game collects no personal data on the open world page, and asks for none. It is meant to be suitable for players aged 13 and over.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host it may log requests (IP address, browser type, time) to operate the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to the developer through the profiles linked from psychobasketball.com.
